drafted 2026-07-29 against one contaminated sample. It has been superseded in
full by **[../pf2sew.md](../pf2sew.md)**, which revises it against the cleaner

both **wrong as written**. `pf2sew.md` §7 corrects the cost estimate ("this
section originally called that 'larger than the emitter'; it is not") and §8
